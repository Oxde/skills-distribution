# Paid marketplaces

Fees and terms change often; the numbers below were read from each marketplace's own pages on the date in SKILL.md. Re-read the seller page before quoting a number to the user.

| Marketplace | How to list | Seller keeps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agensi** (agensi.io/sell) | Creator dashboard | 70% (flat 30% fee) | Free, one-time and subscription pricing. Payout by Stripe or USDC on Solana. 8-point security scan; creators are vetted. |
| **SkillHQ** (skillhq.dev) | `skillhq publish` | 85% minus payment processing | No listing fee. Minimum price €2.00, free allowed. Automated structure, content and security checks. Fingerprinted downloads. Also accepts Custom GPT configs. |
| **MCP Market** (mcpmarket.com/sell) | Import from GitHub or upload SKILL.md | Not stated on the seller page | Storefront plus marketplace listing; GitHub sync with automatic versioning. Ask for the fee before listing. |

Others seen but not checked: PromptBase (accepts SKILL.md products), Skillzy, SkillStack, iUseAgent.

## Advice to give the user

- **A generic skill will not sell.** Buyers can ask an agent to draft one. What sells is a complete, demonstrated workflow from someone whose results they trust: examples, required tools, known limits, updates.
- **Selling direct keeps the most.** A marketplace takes 15–30% and still expects the creator to bring the buyers. If the user has an audience, their own page plus a payment link comes first, and marketplaces are extra reach.
- **Free and paid can coexist.** A free skill on the open registries that does one part well is the best advert for a paid pack.
- **Check exclusivity and licence terms** before listing the same skill in several stores, and never list on ClawHub (MIT-0) something the user intends to sell.
